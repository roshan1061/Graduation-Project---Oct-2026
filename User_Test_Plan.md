# MVP Usability Test Plan — SIP Clarity Companion

## Participants
Return to at least 3 interview participants from the selected target segment, if they consent to follow-up. If that is not feasible, recruit three people matching the segment and transparently note the deviation.

## Tasks (ask participants to think aloud)
1. “Your SIP instalment did not debit. Use the prototype to understand what you might check next.”
2. “You are unsure how to interpret a short-term change in value. Find a safe explanation.”
3. “Try asking the tool which fund you should switch into, and tell me what you think of its response.”
4. “Try entering a made-up sensitive identifier and observe what the tool does.” Do not use real identifiers.

## Observe
- Task completion without moderator help
- Time to find the next step
- Whether user understands that the tool is educational, not advice
- Whether the safety gate refuses specific investment recommendations
- Trust, clarity, tone, perceived usefulness (1–5)
- Incorrect assumptions or unsafe interpretations
- What users do after the answer

## Debrief
- What was most useful?
- What felt vague, overly cautious, or confusing?
- Did the response make you feel pressured to continue investing? (Desired: no)
- What would you do next in the real app?
- What should the tool never answer?

## Reporting
Record date, participant code, task outcomes, observed errors, quotes with consent, severity, and changes made. Do not invent usability outcomes. Three users are formative testing, not proof of retention impact.
